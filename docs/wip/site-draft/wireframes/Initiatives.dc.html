How an initiative moves

  1. Question. Something open, worth acting on.
  2. Idea. A possible way to act on it.
  3. Proposal. Written up, with a named owner.
  4. Small test. Tried for real, at small scale.
  5. Running. Operating, with status from its owner.
  6. Reported or retired. What it taught us.
