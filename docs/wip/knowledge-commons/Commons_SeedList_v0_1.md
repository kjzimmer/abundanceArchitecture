# [COMMONS NAME]: starter seed list

Draft 0.1, 18 Oct 2026. Claude's draft for Karl Zimmer to cut, add to and reorder before it goes into the commons repository as `growth/seed-list`.

**Read this first.**

- These citations are from Claude's memory and **have not been checked**. The acquisition loop looks each one up and records what it actually finds; correct anything it can't match.
- "Likely rights" is a guess to help prioritize, not a finding. Acquisition sets the real rights status. Most books here are under copyright: the commons will hold their citation, summary, short quotations and link, and they go on the permission log.
- The list deliberately includes the strongest opposing classics (for example Hardin), so the commons does not start out one-sided.
- Weighting toward works the commons can hold in full (open access, freely licensed, or likely to grant permission) makes Holon useful sooner. Discovery will also find open versions, such as authors' preprints, of some paywalled papers.

Likely rights: **Open** = probably openly available or licensed; **Ask** = under copyright but a good candidate for a permission request (papers by living authors, institutional reports); **Cite** = under copyright, citation and summary only unless permission comes.

## Gaps in this list

- Practitioner and experiential accounts, which are in scope but hard to find through searches; best gathered by asking contributors.
- Non-Western traditions of holistic thought.
- Money and finance as complex systems beyond Booth.
- Works critical of complexity and living-systems approaches themselves, beyond Hardin. These matter as much as the classics in favour.
